A Message

From the Founder’s Pen

Dear Friends,

Social and cultural development is a process through which structural reorganization is influenced over time. It therefore aims at building a form and structure through which the holistic development of a society can take place.

In reality, the social and cultural development of human beings becomes possible through education. Education develops knowledge, experience and skills, resulting in the reconstruction of personal and social life. Thus, education is the main activity of life.

To fulfil this very objective, the vision of Shri Chandrika Singh Social and Cultural Foundation is to take initiatives towards uplifting the quality of education culturally.

Social health, or harmony, refers to the unity and coordination among people — the principal members of society — the individual, and the world in which they live. We must understand the aspects that shape the objectives and vision of this organization.

The social and cultural concept of health and thought is largely based on an individual’s ability to deal with life’s experiences. The mere presence of medically defined symptoms need not prevent a person from performing daily activities.

Thus, we can become socially healthy only when we are individually healthy. To fulfil this objective, this organization will soon move forward towards providing healthcare facilities at a medical level and through awareness programmes.

Friends, through the “Chatar Mahotsav” to be organized on the coming 28th of November, we will present the vision and mission of the Foundation before you and formally initiate our institutional activities. Your presence on this occasion is sincerely desired.

Ramayan Singh
Shri Ramayan Singh
Co-Founder, Shri Chandrika Singh Social & Cultural Foundation

Our Heritage

The Legacy of Shri Chandrika Singh

“A Foundation dedicated to future generations — inspired by our ancestors.”

If we ask you who developed human civilization on this earth, your one-line answer would be — our ancestors. It is because of them that we have built such a beautiful world. They created food to eat, houses to live in, clothes to cover the body, and villages and cities in which to survive.

Even while knowing that nothing in this world is more powerful than nature, our ancestors fought against nature itself to protect us. Today’s science, too, is the knowledge of our ancestors — this we all know. But the question is: how much have we been able to preserve the great heritage of our ancestors, which is our legacy?

Is it not our responsibility to fulfil not only our own dreams, but also the dreams of the ancestors from whom we first learned to dream?

The family of Shri Chandrika Singh, a resident of Chatar village in the Ara district of Bihar, is an example of a family that is not only preserving the heritage of its ancestors but is also working to fulfil the dreams they once envisioned. Shri Chandrika Singh Social and Cultural Foundation is an organized effort in this very direction.

Shri Chandrika Singh, after whom this Foundation has been established, was the son of Babu Harikishun Singh of “Barka Angna” in Chatar village. Because of his truthfulness and purity, society honoured him with the title of “Sati Baba.” Even today, the elder generation of the village remembers him by that very name. The ideals of Shri Chandrika Singh are the foundation pillars of this organization.

Originally a farmer and householder — yet a devotee of Aghad Paramhans Ram — Shri Chandrika Singh raised two sons, Shri Ram Ayodhya Singh and Shri Baleshwar Singh, who each established their own distinct identity among different sections of society.

05
A Family of Service

The Next Generations

Shri Ram Ayodhya Singh — The Elder Son

The elder son, Shri Ram Ayodhya Singh, was known as a strict and upright police officer, and was honoured with medals for his bravery. Alongside his service, he held a deep love for Bhojpuri songs and music. The songs of Bhikhari Thakur, Mahendra Misir and Rasool Miya were forever on his lips, and he remained present at most musical and cultural gatherings.

Shri Baleshwar Singh — The Younger Son

The younger son, Shri Baleshwar Singh, built his identity as a teacher. A Hindi teacher at Arya Vidyalaya in Titagarh, North 24 Parganas, West Bengal, he contributed greatly to the promotion of Hindi while living in Bengal. He also provided free tuition and primary education to thousands of children from Hindi-speaking labour communities. Today, the students he taught are doing remarkable work across many fields of society.

In this way, the present generation of Shri Chandrika Singh’s family has established this Foundation to preserve the heritage of their ancestors and, inspired by their ideals and vision, to work collectively across different fields of society.

Founder member Shri Ramayan Singh says that through this organization, the family will work in the days to come across many fields — foremost among them education, healthcare and social service.

Among these, the most important is to arrange advanced education for poor children through the Foundation. As Shri Ramayan Singh puts it: “It is the inspiration of our ancestors that balanced and meaningful development of society is possible only when children from every section of society receive quality education. Our ancestors tried to do this individually; now we will make organizational efforts to fulfil their dreams.”

The Vision in Action

Education & Healthcare

The Foundation’s second great focus is healthcare. In the words of Shri Ramayan Singh:

“We and our ancestors have suffered for the lack of medical treatment at the local level. We will not allow our future generations to suffer this same pain. A medical centre will be built in the village, where expert doctors and medicines will be available 24 hours a day. In the days to come, a large hospital will also be established under this Foundation, where every kind of disease can be treated.”

These commitments — quality education for every child and dependable healthcare close to home — sit at the heart of the Foundation’s work. Around them, six core focus areas guide everything we do:

Education

Quality, skill-based education for underprivileged children.

Healthcare

Medical facilities, awareness and reliable local health services.

Social Service

Support for the poor, orphaned, elderly and the needy.

Skill Development

Training and entrepreneurship for self-reliance.

Youth & Culture

Sports, arts and cultural activity for youth development.

Relief & Rehabilitation

Relief during disasters and community support programmes.

07
Beyond the Classroom

Social Service, Youth & Skill

Another founding member, Shri Ravi Ranjan Singh, explains the Foundation’s social initiatives: “We will not only work in the fields of education and healthcare, but will also work to preserve our social and cultural values.”

In the coming days a Youth Club will be established under the Foundation, uniting the youth of society and nurturing their talent in sports and the arts. Large-scale sports and cultural competitions will be held in the village, with arrangements made so that players and artists receive proper training in their chosen fields.

The Foundation will place special emphasis on the welfare of the poor and underprivileged — arranging marriages for daughters of poor families, caring for the helpless and orphaned, and ensuring basic facilities reach people at the very last rung of society.

“The main reason for poverty in our society is unemployment. So the goal of the Foundation is to prepare our future generations in such a skilled manner that they can easily find employment in every field. We will help as many people as possible find work, and help them understand the intricacies of self-employment before they adopt it.”
— Shri Ravi Ranjan Singh

The formal launch of this skill-training programme will begin in the coming days. The Foundation will be inaugurated on the 28th of November, where — through “Chatar Mahotsav” — we will bring the people of our society together and discuss the Foundation’s future plans and activities in detail.

We warmly request the honour of your presence at Chatar Mahotsav.

— Board of Directors, Shri Chandrika Singh Social & Cultural Foundation

Our Commitments

What Will the Foundation Do?

  1. Arrangement of food for hungry people.
  2. Arrangement of community kitchens.
  3. Relief and rescue work during natural or man-made disasters.
  4. Measures to eradicate leprosy, TB and other serious diseases.
  5. Awareness programmes and preventive measures for physical and mental illness.
  6. Care of orphans and abandoned people through orphanages, shelter homes and old-age homes.
  7. Healthcare services through hospitals, nursing homes and community clinics.
  8. Skill-based education for students from weaker sections through schools, colleges and other institutions.
  9. Awareness on health and well-being for students in schools and colleges, and for daily-wage workers.
  10. Awareness among people below the poverty line on family health, responsible relationships and mental well-being.
  11. Camps to raise mental-health awareness at every level of society — to ease emotional distress, prevent suicidal tendencies and help people cope with rejection.

Our Commitments

What Will the Foundation Do?

  1. Entrepreneurship programmes for youth, women and underprivileged sections of society.
  2. Awareness programmes and training camps in collaboration with government departments and regulatory institutions.
  3. Rehabilitation and reform of prisoners through technical and employment-based education, so they can contribute positively to society.
  4. Providing better sanitation facilities to citizens.
  5. Arranging easy credit facilities for the financial security of low-income groups.
  6. Providing proper, dignified care for terminally ill patients.
  7. Securing government, private, domestic and foreign grants for development activities.
  8. Scholarships for students from weaker income groups and financial assistance for needy individuals and communities.
  9. Helping people express their views firmly yet respectfully — understanding that there can be different perspectives on the same issue and that we need not always agree. We will promote the spirit of accepting and tolerating all ideas, in the belief that people can live in peace and mutual love even while holding strong, differing opinions. Conflict arises from “ego”; it is vital for humanity to set it aside so that all may live peacefully.
  10. Establishing and preserving memoirs, museums, and other forms of art, architecture, monuments and photographs that can stand as a strong historical record for future generations.

Objectives in Detail

Our Vision, Our Mission

  1. To promote, research, support and work towards educational development.
  2. To assist economically weaker and deserving students through educational activities.
  3. To establish, manage and run educational institutions, schools and colleges.
  4. To operate training centres, skill and semi-skill institutes, coaching classes and social-service centres, and to organize seminars and workshops.
  5. To establish digital libraries across the country to support study and research in all branches and faculties, and to organize lectures, meetings and conferences.
  6. To promote knowledge of education and research, technologies, processes and opportunities.
  7. To organize seminars, road shows, lectures, programmes, festivals and conferences for students.
  8. To organize music and entertainment programmes and to invite technocrats, entrepreneurs, leaders, industrialists and scientists.
  9. To acquire or transfer copyrights and other intellectual property from authors, speakers and distinguished personalities, and to publish, sell and distribute books, pamphlets, articles and magazines.
  10. To provide and develop the infrastructure necessary for conducting research and surveys.
  11. To promote the products and processes developed by innovators and entrepreneurs within the organization.
